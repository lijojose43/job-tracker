Questions, answered.

Is my data stored on your servers?+

No. Job Tracker is local-first and free — data lives in your browser with optional export. Nothing is sent to a server.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. It's an installable PWA with a service worker. Add, edit, and search with zero connection — changes persist locally.

Can I migrate from spreadsheets?+

In under a minute. Export your sheet to JSON (or use our template) and import — dedupe is automatic.

Is it really free?+

Yes — free forever. No signup, no credit card, no limits. Open the app and start tracking.
